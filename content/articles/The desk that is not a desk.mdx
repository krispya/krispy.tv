With an undue confidence, she looked at me and said, "you are almost over the hill." The train buzzed and bustled as it usually does, but especially on a Thursday night when you want to have a conversation. We were headed where it does not matter and she was right.

For most of my life I have created and toiled and when the seasons chilled I would begin anew, but rarely was I proud. Even more rarely did I share and so I unfolded plainly, quietly. Being unseen is safer in almost every way — risk forlorn — except that the unseen is unknown even to the self. Looking at my desk with years of wear and various object with various colors, shapes, marks and symbols all about, I see this is impossible. I am a song and the orchestra is in the pit.

And so came into existence this desk that is not a desk filled with thoughts, interests and reveries.

My perceptions of I will always be less than yours.
Allow me to give you a chance.
